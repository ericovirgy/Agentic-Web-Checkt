About Northwind Bikes

Northwind Bikes was founded in 2014 by two mechanics who wanted bikes that are easy to maintain. We design three models and build each one to order in our Portsmouth workshop.

Our team

Eight people work at Northwind: four mechanics, two designers and two in customer service. You can reach all of us at hello@northwind.example.
