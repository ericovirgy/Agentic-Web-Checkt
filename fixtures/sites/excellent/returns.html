Return policy

You can return any bike or accessory within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging.

How to return an item

  1. Email hello@northwind.example with your order number.
  2. We send you a prepaid shipping label within one working day.
  3. Drop the parcel at any courier point. Refunds are issued within 5 working days of receipt.

Exceptions

Custom-painted frames and gift cards cannot be returned. Faulty items can be returned at any time under warranty.
